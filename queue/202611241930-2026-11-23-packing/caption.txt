Packing 50 orders? Stop picking one-by-one. 📦

A smarter packing workflow can save you from unnecessary shelf-walking, repeated SKU checks and daily chaos.

Try batching your orders:

1️⃣ Group labels by SKU
2️⃣ Pick the same product together
3️⃣ Verify size, colour & quantity
4️⃣ Pack the batch
5️⃣ Attach the correct shipping label

Small workflow changes can make your dispatch routine smoother. 🚀
